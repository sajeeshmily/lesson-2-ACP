Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was a famous Indian scientist and the 11th President of India. He was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

Early Life

He came from a humble family and worked hard to achieve his dreams.

Career and Achievements

He contributed to India's space and missile programmes. He became President of India in 2002.

His Message to Students

He encouraged students to dream big and work hard.

A Lasting Legacy

He passed away on 27 July 2015 while delivering a lecture. He is remembered as a great teacher and scientist.
